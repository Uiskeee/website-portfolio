Face Lift

3D Puzzle Game for PC

Overview

Face Lift is a puzzle game where players must steal body parts of the face in order to construct one for themselves to blend in. They must then gather information information from people around them to sneak in and find their target while also making sure their poorly made disguise doesn't droop too much!

Although the game is currently buggy and has no real ending, the core feature of customisation and puzzle solving is can still be experienced!


Face Lift was made for Global Game Jam 2026 over the course of 2 days in a team of 4, including 2 artists and 2 programmers.

My Role

I developed the core mechanic of putting together a a disguise, including the UI, judgement system and gathering of parts. Along with the other UI elements and integration of art assets.

Technical Challenges

As this was my first game jam, working under a tight schedule of 2 days taught me a lot on what was truly needed in a project and what could be left behind.

Something I also learnt was working in a fast-pased environment with others, having to constantly communicate and work together in the short time that we had.
