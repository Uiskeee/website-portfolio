Overview

Great Wisdom has the player play as a knight traversing through the dungeon, with a twist! The player can rotate the world, allowing them to traverse various levels and reach the end in order to gain a wish. This wish will not come easy however, and the player must prove themselves.


Great Wisdom is a group project made for my school module, Software Project 1, with a group of 4 programmers, including me.

My Role

  • Project Manager: Assigned work and managed communication between developers.
  • Lead Gameplay Programmer: Developed a framework for use by other developers.
  • Level Designer: Designed the various levels used in the game.

    • Technical Challenges

    Being put in a position where I am working with developers with less experience made it so I would have to explain many terms and structures I was already familiar with.

    As it had been a while since I had done a project in C++ and not a commercial engine, it helped me recall and fortify my basics greatly.
